Environmental Health & Allergies

Asthma

Can be affected by pollution, pollen, smoke and cold air.

Symptoms:

Coughing, wheezing and breathing difficulties.

Hay Fever

Often triggered by pollen from grass, trees and weeds.

Symptoms:

Sneezing, runny nose and itchy eyes.

Eczema

Weather changes, dry air and pollution may trigger symptoms.

Symptoms:

Dry, itchy and irritated skin.

Allergic Rhinitis

Can be caused by pollen, dust, mould and other allergens.

Symptoms:

Sneezing, blocked nose and itchy eyes.
